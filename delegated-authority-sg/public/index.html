Delegated Financial Authority · Singapore

When someone loses capacity,
their family shouldn't lose visibility.

A Singpass-style delegated authority concept for care scenarios in Singapore. It allows an authorised person to switch profile context and access only approved, view-only data.

Today — broken for families
  • •A parent has a stroke, but the family has no visibility into insurance or CPF position.
  • •Critical journeys usually require the principal to complete live Singpass authentication.
  • •Caregivers cannot quickly estimate affordability, premiums, or support eligibility.
  • •Decisions are delayed because essential financial context is fragmented.
With Delegated Authority
  • •The principal nominates an authorised delegate via OPG/LPA workflow.
  • •The delegate signs in with their own Singpass and switches to Delegated Authority profile.
  • •QR login shows only approved fields such as MediSave or policy summary data.
  • •Every delegated access is audit-logged and visible to both principal and delegate.

How it works

1
Pre-authorise
Principal logs in via Singpass and grants named delegate access to specific data fields
2
Crisis happens
Stroke, dementia onset, severe injury — principal can no longer perform live biometric login
3
Switch to delegated profile
Authorised person opens their own Singpass app and switches profile from Principal to Delegated Authority
4
Scan and access scoped data
Using delegated profile, QR login to CPF/insurers is view-only and restricted (e.g. MediSave balance, policy/premium summary)

This is a proof-of-concept powered by MockPass (free local Singpass simulator). No real personal data is used.
